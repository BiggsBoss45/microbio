GRAM STAINING PROCEDURE

1. Take a microscope slide and, using the Q-tip in the vial, create circular smear (filled in circle) in the middle of the slide.
2. Wait until the smear appears fogged up/dry.
3. Using the lighter, grab the slide and wave up it above the flame three times. Do not let the slide touch the flame.
4. Apply Crystal Violet to the smear and wait 60 seconds.
5. Tilt the slide and,using the pipette, wash the slide by applying water to the top end of the slide until the majory of the Crystal Violet is washed away.
6. Apply Iodine Solution to the smear and wait 60 seconds
7. Tilt the slide and,using the pipette, wash the slide by applying water to the top end of the slide until the majory of the Iodine is washed away.
8. Tilt the slide and apply alcohol to the slide until the whole slide has been washed.
9. Apply Safranin Counterstain to the smear and wait 60 seconds.
10. Tilt the slide and,using the pipette, wash the slide by applying water to the top end of the slide until the majory of the Safranin is washed away.
11. Dry the slide and place a slide cover to the smear. 12. When using the microscope make sure that the lens does NOT touch the slide.
